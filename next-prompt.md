A high-converting Landing Page for "PolicySathi", an insurance comparison and management platform.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- **Personality:** Premium, Trustworthy, Modern, Transparent
- **Target Audience:** Indian families looking for accessible and secure policy management
- **Primary (Sapphire):** `#0A192F` (Deep, secure financial feel)
- **Secondary (Emerald):** `#10B981` (Growth, safety, success)
- **Background:** `#FFFFFF` or very soft gray `#F8FAFC`
- **Text (Primary):** `#1E293B`
- **Font Family:** `Inter`, `Outfit`, or `Roboto`
- **UI Elements:** Soft drop shadows (`shadow-sm` or `shadow-md`), subtle borders (`border-slate-200`), rounded corners (`rounded-xl` or `rounded-2xl`).

**Page Structure:**
1. A modern sticky Navigation Bar (Logo left, Links center, Login/Signup right).
2. A large Hero section with a strong headline ("Secure Your Family's Future with PolicySathi"), a subheadline, a powerful Call-to-Action button ("Get a Free Quote"), and an engaging abstract vector graphic or hero image placeholder.
3. Just below the hero, a dynamic horizontal ticker (Marquee) showcasing logos of top Indian Insurance Companies (e.g., LIC, HDFC Life, SBI Life, ICICI Prudential, Max Life) to build trust.
4. "Why Choose Us" section with 3 premium feature cards (e.g., "100% Transparent", "Fastest Claims", "Lowest Premiums").
5. A dark minimal Footer.
